# Changelog

One line per change a caller can see. Versions are the `v*` tags `tag-main.yml` cuts on every merge to `dev` (a `feat`
raises the minor version); the release notes on each tag list every merged pull request.

## v1.30.1 (REL-FAST-2, RV-112)
- `release-ecs.yml`: the `verify` job is folded into `finish` (one job fewer, about a minute of runner pick-up off every release; 8 jobs, the backend runs at most 7, web 5). Both ran on the stateful class with the same role and lock token; verify's steps run first in `finish`, each gated on the job env `VERIFY_RUNS` (verify's old `if`: never a dry run; the deploy job succeeded, or failed once the rollout had started), then the deployment record and the unlock with `always()` as before. Same smoke, digest check, deploy-alarm check, rollback (with its fresh role session), issue, release-lock end record, put-back and unlock; every long step keeps its own limit, so a hang ends as a failed step and the unlock still runs (job limit 120 = 60 + 10 + 40 + 10 untimed; `TIMEOUT_EXCEPTIONS` and the limits table follow). The outputs `watch`, `rollback`, `ready` move to `finish` (`needs.finish.outputs.*` in `api-client` and `notify`). `api-client` (its own `packages: write`) and `notify` (fail-open, small class, after `api-client`) stay jobs of their own.
- `scripts/notify_release.py` (RV-112 S1/S2): a SUCCESS notice of `release-ecs.yml` now says `rolled out; after the bake a deploy alarm only notifies (no automatic rollback): it posts to <channel>`; the channel is the new optional input `alerts-channel` (no default; preprod's deploy alarms go to `#alerts-critical`, onepeek-infra `config/preprod.yaml` `slack_critical_alarms`), unset it says `the alerts channel`. Callers pass `alerts-channel: '#alerts-critical'` when their pin moves; no other input, secret or caller change.

## v1.30.0 (REL-FAST, founder 2026-11-18)
- `release-ecs.yml` verify: the fixed 10-minute deploy-alarm watch is gone. Each alarmed service already rolls back on its own two deploy alarms (ECS deployment alarms with rollback), and the rollout's wait ends only at rolloutState COMPLETED, after ECS's own bake, failing on any ECS rollback (verify then puts every service back, as before). The `watch` step (id and output kept) is now one check after the smoke checks that none of the rolled services' deploy alarms is firing (step 16 -> 8 minutes, verify job 80 -> 70). About 10 minutes off every backend and web release.
- `scripts/notify_release.py`: a SUCCESS notice of `release-ecs.yml` (WATCH set) adds `rolled out, watching: a deploy alarm that fires from now on posts to #alerts`: the deploy alarms stay on and notify the alerts topic, which infra's slack-alerts function posts to #alerts (aws-stack §10.12). No new path. No input, output, secret or job changes: callers pick it up when their pin moves.

## v1.29.2 (F661)
- fix: a rollout no longer waits on ECS's own `aws ecs wait services-stable` (40 x 15 s = 10 minutes, shorter than a preprod order-1 rollout plus the deploy-alarm bake time; backend release #149). New shared `rollout_wait <cap> <service>=<expected revision | ->...` (a `stable-wait` block, identical in every copy: in each release-lock block of `release-ecs.yml`, `operate.yml` and `release-lock.yml`, and in `release-ecs.yml`'s rollout step; `scripts/check_service_list.py` checks the copies) polls describe-services every 15 s. A service is done only when its PRIMARY deployment runs the expected revision (the one just registered, or the one put back), is COMPLETED (without a rolloutState: running = desired) and is the only deployment left; `-` (a service scaled to 0) is done once nothing runs. A deployment of the expected revision that is FAILED, or a PRIMARY on another revision once the expected one was listed, is a rollback by ECS (circuit breaker or deploy alarm) and fails at once; a poll before the new deployment is listed waits. Any failure and the cap print every deployment's revision, state and reason, the counts and the 15 newest events, and say which happened. An unreadable describe-services is never success (three in a row fail the wait).
- `release-ecs.yml`: each order waits up to 25 minutes, all orders within 40 (rollout step limit 20 -> 45, deploy job 110 -> 135); verify's rollback prints each service and the revision it puts it on and waits up to 20 minutes (step 20 -> 25, verify job 60 -> 70). The put-back of the lock functions waits up to 20 minutes, as its two 10-minute waits did, for the revision it put back; its failure says "did not become stable (see above)". `operate.yml`: the redeploy waits up to 20 minutes (operate job 30 -> 45). No input, output, secret, job or step name changes: callers pick it up when their pin moves.

## v1.29.1 (SW-GITHUB: F621, F630, F650, F647)
- fix: `release-ecs.yml` verify's rollback step puts a service back through the lock functions' `lock_live_revision` (F621), as the `finish` put-back does since v1.28.4: a recorded revision deregistered since (INACTIVE) is registered again from its recorded definition and tags and the service moves to it, instead of a red rollback that only `finish` recovered; a revision it cannot make active fails the step closed (the key stays `rolling`, `finish` tries again). ## v1.29.0 (W1-SLACK-GH)
- Release notices to Slack #deploys (founder decision 2026-11-15; rules in `release-ecs.yml`'s header): `release-ecs.yml` gains an optional secret `SLACK_DEPLOYS_WEBHOOK` and a last job `notify` that posts ONE message per release at its end (`[<repo>] <version> -> <env>: SUCCESS | FAILED | ROLLED BACK | CANCELLED`, short commit, duration, the `/api/status` or `/sign-in` readiness result, the failed job and step, links to the run, the pull request and the commit); none at the start, none for a dry run or a skipped (older) commit. New `release-notify.yml` does the same as the last job of a release that does not run `release-ecs.yml` (the infra release). Both run one shared notifier, `scripts/notify_release.py`, read at the workflow's own commit. It never fails or slows a release: GitHub-hosted, at most 3 minutes, `continue-on-error`; an unset secret is one notice, a Slack error or timeout (curl `--max-time 10`, one retry) one warning; the webhook URL is never printed. `verify` gains the output `ready` (the readiness step's outcome). Callers pass `SLACK_DEPLOYS_WEBHOOK: ${{ secrets.SLACK_DEPLOYS_WEBHOOK }}` when their pin moves to the tag cut from this merge; without it nothing is posted.

## v1.28.4 (W1-GH: F589, C11)
- `release-ecs.yml`, `operate.yml`, `release-lock.yml` (the shared lock functions): the put-back of a `rolling` key no longer fails on a recorded revision that was deregistered since (an infra apply replacing a skeleton revision leaves it INACTIVE, and ECS refuses `UpdateService` onto it): it describes the revision, registers it again from its recorded definition and tags, and moves the service to the new revision. A revision it cannot describe, of another family, with no container, in any other status, or whose registration fails or answers another family, fails the put-back closed (the key stays `rolling`, as before). The deploy role needs `ecs:DescribeTaskDefinition` and `ecs:RegisterTaskDefinition` (it already has both for the rollout).
- `release-ecs.yml` verify: when none of the rolled services' deploy alarms exists, the 10-minute watch is skipped with the same warning (it could not detect a regression) instead of waiting 10 minutes; with any alarm it watches as before. The step now sets `pipefail` itself (it has no `shell:`, so the runner runs it as `bash -e {0}`), so an unreadable alarm list, at the start or in any poll of the window, fails the step and rolls back instead of reading as "no alarm". Callers pick both up when their pin moves to the tag cut from this merge.
